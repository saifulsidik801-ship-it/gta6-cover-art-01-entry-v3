Vice Cover Studio — turn your photos into GTA 6-style cover art: a 7-panel Vice City sunset poster with a huge striped gradient title, neon script subtitle, role labels, location chip, starburst seal, palm strip and catalogue footer. 8 layouts × 6 colour grades × 6 caption presets, drag-to-pan and scroll-to-zoom per panel, one-click shuffle, and PNG export at 1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1920×1080 and 1080×1920. Fully offline — photos never leave your machine, no Rockstar assets, OFL fonts bundled.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
